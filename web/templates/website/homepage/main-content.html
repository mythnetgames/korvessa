Korvessa is a walled farm community, built on the labor of its citizens and the rhythm of the land. Fields and gardens surround the city, and most families work together to grow, harvest, and share what they can. The city is known for its strong sense of cooperation and the cycles of planting and reaping that shape daily life.

Every year, the city gathers for the Sprinkling, a holiday marking the end of the planting season and the beginning of the harvest. Neighbors share food, stories, and music in the central square, and children run through the fields with ribbons and bells. The Sprinkling is a time of hope and gratitude, a reminder that survival depends on both luck and hard work. Five years ago, the Sprinkling was interrupted, and since then, the city has faced new uncertainties and shifting alliances.

One hundred and sixty years ago, cataclysms drove humanity into five sacred cities. Since The Herding, Korvessa has endured, isolated, protected, and bound by law and tradition. The boundaries beyond the city remain deadly; none who venture beyond and attempt to escape return alive.

What will you become in this walled world?

Life in Korvessa

Every day offers choices. Here are some of the paths people walk:

Farm and Labor

Work the fields, tend gardens, harvest crops. Build your reputation through honest labor and skill.

Trading and Chance

Buy, sell, gamble, or barter. Take risks in the markets and see what fortune brings.

Crafting and Repair

Learn to craft, repair tools, forge weapons, or mend clothes. Skill earns respect.

Healing and Care

Learn medicine, tend wounds, and help the sick. Be trusted by your community.

Conflict and Combat

Test yourself in duels, settle disputes, or protect what matters. Risk brings consequence.

Politics and Influence

Build alliances, sway opinion, gather followers. Shape the city through words and deeds.

Paths in Korvessa

Korvessa is a city of choices, constraints, and consequences. Every citizen must decide what kind of story they want to live, and what risks, duties, or ambitions they are willing to accept. The walls keep out the unknown, but inside, every day is shaped by the paths people choose and the limits they embrace.

Order and Commitment

Some in Korvessa find meaning in discipline, service, and the pursuit of virtue. They choose codes, accept limitations, and believe that meaning is earned by carrying a single story to its end. What does it mean to commit to a path when other options remain open? Is virtue found in the act of choosing, or in the endurance to see it through?

Chance and Risk

Others embrace luck, risk, and the thrill of uncertainty. They gamble, perform, and take chances, believing that freedom is found in not knowing the outcome until it happens. Is true rebellion found in risking what cannot be predicted? What is the value of freedom if you know the end before you begin?

Ambition and Self-Authorship

Some seek to assert their will, shape their own fate, and bend the world to their vision. They believe that the act of choosing and forcing a single outcome is the only real freedom. Is power simply the ability to choose and persist, or is it another kind of constraint? What does it mean to author your own story in a world full of other ambitions?

In Korvessa, most citizens cannot explain the philosophies behind their choices, but they live them every day. They swear oaths, take risks, seize control, and sometimes simply survive. The city does not promise better outcomes; it promises a single story you can live inside, and that is the closest thing to freedom anyone has.
